      How much spend goes on people Kukoon already has?

      Northbeam can't prove incrementality; that needs a holdout test. It can show where spend lands on people who already know or buy from Kukoon, which is where non-incremental spend hides.

      Spend by campaign new-customer share

      Spend by campaign % new visits

      Campaigns ranked by spend reaching existing audiences

      Spend on returning customers = spend × returning share of customers. Flags: new visits under 30%, new-customer share under 70%, or recent-repeat customers above 10%.

      What Northbeam can and can't tell you

      • Can: who each campaign's clicks and buyers are (new, returning, recent repeat). That shows where spend is likely to be low-incrementality.
      • Can't: whether those people would have bought anyway. A returning visitor clicking a Shopping ad may have been about to come back through brand search or email.
      • To prove it: run a holdout. Either a Google conversion-lift study or a geo split, for example pausing IE Shopping in half of the Republic for 4 weeks. Compare total orders, not attributed orders.
